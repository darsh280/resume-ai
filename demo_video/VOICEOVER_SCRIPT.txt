HOW TO RECORD:
1. Open your phone voice recorder (or Audacity / Voice Recorder on Windows).
2. Play the video 'resume_ai_demo.mp4' on screen.
3. Read the script lines below following each scene's timestamp.
4. Save the recording as 'my_voice.mp3' in the demo_video folder.
5. Run: python add_voiceover.py
It will automatically merge your voice with the 1080p video!
